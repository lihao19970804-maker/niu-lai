# Community code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community.
Examples of positive behavior include using inclusive language, respecting
different viewpoints, giving specific and constructive feedback, accepting
responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what benefits the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, sexualized language or attention, publishing another person's private
information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
VigilMesh. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these
standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the primary maintainer through their
GitHub profile. Do not disclose another person's private information in a
public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and as confidentially as
practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.
